Frequently asked questions

How does Arkansas's CPA license renewal cycle work?

Annual. All Arkansas individual CPA/PA licenses expire December 31 of each year and renew annually for a one-year term.

How much does it cost to renew a CPA license in Arkansas?

$110. $110 annual renewal fee for an individual active license to practice, per the Board's own current rules (17 CAR 236-1101(c)(3)). Late renewal adds $25 per month. Note a widely-cited $150 figure is the REINSTATEMENT fee in the same rule, not the renewal fee.

Does Arkansas require CPE hours to renew a CPA license?

Yes -- Arkansas requires 120 hours of CPE every 3 years. That includes 4 ethics hours. See the full Arkansas CPE requirements page for the exact citation.

What does it cost to reinstate a lapsed Arkansas CPA license?

$150. Flat, non-refundable $150 reinstatement fee (17 CAR § 236-1101) if reinstated within 3 years of expiration -- distinct from the separate $25/month late-renewal fee that accrues in the first 3 months after the December 31 expiration, before the license actually lapses.

Does a CPA firm need to register separately in Arkansas?

Yes -- Arkansas has its own firm-level registration or permit requirement, separate from an individual CPA's license renewal. See the full Arkansas firm renewal page for the exact cycle and citation.
