Does your firm need to register here?

A different question from the check above: does your FIRM itself need to register in a state where it has no office, for attest work you're doing there? Firms sometimes assume that if the individual CPA has practice privilege, the firm is covered too — the firm often still has its own separate registration requirement, and that gap is one of the most common real-world mobility mistakes.
