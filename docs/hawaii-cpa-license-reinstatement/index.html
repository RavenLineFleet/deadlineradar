Hawaii CPA License Reinstatement: What a Lapsed License Costs

If your Hawaii CPA license has already lapsed

What it actually takes to get a lapsed Hawaii CPA license back — the fee, the catch-up CPE, and exactly what triggers "lapsed" in the first place. Sourced the same way every fact on this site is: a board page, plus the codified rule itself where we could confirm it against primary law, clearly labelled where we could only confirm it against the board's own page, never a guess.

Reinstatement Fee
$204

Board's own fee page: $204 to restore a lapsed individual license, vs. $152 for on-time renewal. Firm permit-to-practice restoration is separate and higher ($360 entity / $128 sole proprietor).

Catch-Up CPE Required
  • HAR §16-71-35 requires making up any CPE deficiency from the standard 80-hour biennial requirement before the Board approves the permit to practice -- completion of an existing shortfall, not an additional penalty on top of the normal requirement.
Source of record Hawaii Administrative Rules Title 16, Chapter 71, §16-71-2 (biennial renewal) and §16-71-35 (deficiency and carryover hours) read the rule →

Sourcing note: The $204/$360/$128 fee figures are confirmed only on the Board's own page -- HAR Chapter 71 defers the actual renewal/restoration fee amounts to a separate fee-schedule chapter (16-53) that could not be located at a working URL. Treat the dollar figures as board-confirmed but not yet codified-rule-confirmed until that chapter is located and cited directly. (Last checked 2026-08-20.)

What triggers lapsed status: Licenses and permits renew biennially by December 31 of odd-numbered years. Per the Board's own page, licenses not renewed by the expiration date are unlicensed and may not practice.
