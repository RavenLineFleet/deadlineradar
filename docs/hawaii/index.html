Frequently asked questions

How does Hawaii's CPA license renewal cycle work?

Biennial. The CPA license (HRS § 466-5(j)) and the individual and firm permits to practice (HRS § 466-7) all expire December 31 of every odd-numbered year -- the same fixed date across all three credential types, no cohort split.

How much does it cost to renew a CPA license in Hawaii?

$152. $52 biennial individual CPA license renewal fee (HAR §16-53-38(7)(A)) plus a mandatory $100 biennial Compliance Resolution Fund fee (HAR §16-53-38(12)(A)(iii)) -- $152 total each biennial renewal.

Does Hawaii require CPE hours to renew a CPA license?

Yes -- Hawaii requires 80 hours of CPE every 2 years. That includes 4 ethics hours. See the full Hawaii CPE requirements page for the exact citation.

What does it cost to reinstate a lapsed Hawaii CPA license?

$204. Board's own fee page: $204 to restore a lapsed individual license, vs. $152 for on-time renewal. Firm permit-to-practice restoration is separate and higher ($360 entity / $128 sole proprietor).

Does a CPA firm need to register separately in Hawaii?

Yes -- Hawaii has its own firm-level registration or permit requirement, separate from an individual CPA's license renewal. See the full Hawaii firm renewal page for the exact cycle and citation.
