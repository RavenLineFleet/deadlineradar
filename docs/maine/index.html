Frequently asked questions

How does Maine's CPA license renewal cycle work?

The Board's own page states both individual CPA licenses and firm licenses renew annually on September 30, and the codified rule independently confirms all licenses expire annually with no cohort split -- but the codified rule never states the specific September 30 date itself.

How much does it cost to renew a CPA license in Maine?

$35. Annual renewal fee of $35 (renewal is annual, expiring September 30); $50 late fee if renewed after expiration.

Does Maine require CPE hours to renew a CPA license?

Yes -- Maine requires 40 hours of CPE every 1 year. That includes 4 ethics hours. See the full Maine CPE requirements page for the exact citation.

What does it cost to reinstate a lapsed Maine CPA license?

$35. Reinstatement applications are charged the standard $35/yr CPA license fee (02-041 C.M.R. ch.10 Sec5(1)) -- Maine has no separate reinstatement-specific fee line item. A late-renewal cure within 90 days of expiration instead draws a separate $50 uniform late fee (02-041 C.M.R. ch.11 Sec2(1)).

Does a CPA firm need to register separately in Maine?

Yes -- Maine has its own firm-level registration or permit requirement, separate from an individual CPA's license renewal. See the full Maine firm renewal page for the exact cycle and citation.
