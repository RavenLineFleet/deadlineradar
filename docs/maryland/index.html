Frequently asked questions

How does Maryland's CPA license renewal cycle work?

Biennial (2-year) renewal, but your exact expiration date is personal to your license, not one shared date. Maryland's statute (Md. Code Ann., Bus. Occ. & Prof. 2-311(a)(2)) sets a default: unless renewed for a 2-year term, a license expires on the first December 31 that comes after the license's effective date and falls in an even-numbered year. That default applies only when no staggering determination is in force under the Secretary's separate authority (2-311(e)). The Board's own renewal-guidance page has published a fixed PERSONAL month/day anniversary (worked example: a license issued January 2 expiring January 2) continuously and unchanged for over a decade, and a review of active, current Maryland CPA license records -- sourced from the Board's own licensee database via NASBA's CPAverify lookup -- confirms that personal-anniversary pattern is genuinely in force today: real, currently active licenses expire on their own month/day anniversary, including dates outside December and in odd-numbered years. Not every record follows it, though -- at least one currently active license instead shows exactly the statutory December-31/even-year date -- so which date governs your license depends on your own record. 80 hours of qualifying CE (incl. 4 hours ethics) is required at the second and subsequent renewals; the first renewal after initial licensure is exempt from the CE-hour requirement. No separate CPE-reporting deadline -- CE is self-reported directly on the renewal application, due on the licensee's own expiration date.

How much does it cost to renew a CPA license in Maryland?

$63. Individual CPA renewal fee is $63 (firm renewal is separately $151).

Does Maryland require CPE hours to renew a CPA license?

Yes, Maryland requires 80 hours of CPE every 2 years. That includes 4 ethics hours. See the full Maryland CPE requirements page for the exact citation.

What does it cost to reinstate a lapsed Maryland CPA license?

$126. Double the standard $63 renewal fee (COMAR 09.24.01.09.B(9)/(4)). Charged as soon as the license's 2-year expiration date passes; the Board's FAQ states it currently cannot be waived.

Does a CPA firm need to register separately in Maryland?

Yes, Maryland has its own firm-level registration or permit requirement, separate from an individual CPA's license renewal. See the full Maryland firm renewal page for the exact cycle and citation.
