Ohio CPA License Reinstatement: What a Lapsed License Costs

If your Ohio CPA license has already lapsed

What it actually takes to get a lapsed Ohio CPA license back — the fee, the catch-up CPE, and exactly what triggers "lapsed" in the first place. Sourced the same way every fact on this site is: a board page, plus the codified rule itself where we could confirm it against primary law, clearly labelled where we could only confirm it against the board's own page, never a guess.

Reinstatement Fee

No flat fee -- a monthly late-filing charge under ORC 4701.10(D)-(E): up to $100/month (cap $1,200) for a practicing permit holder, up to $50/month (cap $300) for a registration/non-practicing holder. If the Board finds 'excusable neglect' (ORC 4701.10(F)), it may instead set a reduced fee (not exceeding $50 plus the standard triennial permit fee per lapsed period).

Catch-Up CPE Required
  • 120 CPE hours Same 120-credit/3-year total as ordinary renewal (Ohio Admin. Code 4701-15-02(A)), but reinstatement (OAC 4701-15-09) requires all 120 within the 36 months immediately preceding the application AND subject-matter minimums ordinary renewal doesn't impose: at least 24 credits in accounting/auditing/assurance/attestation, at least 24 in taxation.
  • 3 ethics hours, within that total.
Source of record Ohio Rev. Code § 4701.10(A),(B),(D),(E),(F); Ohio Admin. Code 4701-15-02(D), 4701-15-09 (120 credits/36 months, 24 A&A, 24 tax, 3 ethics) read the rule →

Sourcing note: No flat reinstatement fee is published because Ohio's own law sets a formula/cap, not a flat number, stated in plain terms above instead. The Board's own fees page is live and was read directly, including its "Late and Back Fees" tab: "Late fees for expired licenses accrue at a rate of $50/month (to a maximum amount of $300) for Registrations and Permits, and $100/month (to a maximum amount of $1,200) for Permit - Employed in Public Accounting" -- which is where the figures above come from. (Last checked 2026-08-20.)

What triggers lapsed status: Permits/registrations expire December 31 on a staggered triennial cohort cycle (not one date for all licensees). Failing to renew within ONE YEAR of that expiration date suspends the underlying CPA certificate/PA registration (ORC 4701.10(F)) until accrued late fees are paid or a reduced fee is approved.
