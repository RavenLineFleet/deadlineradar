Oregon CPA License Reinstatement: What a Lapsed License Costs

If your Oregon CPA license has already lapsed

What it actually takes to get a lapsed Oregon CPA license back — the fee, the catch-up CPE, and exactly what triggers "lapsed" in the first place. Sourced the same way every fact on this site is: a board page, plus the codified rule itself where we could confirm it against primary law, clearly labelled where we could only confirm it against the board's own page, never a guess.

Reinstatement Fee

Formula: a $225 non-refundable reinstatement application fee (OAR 801-010-0010) plus the standard $255 active biennial renewal fee for each lapsed renewal period -- total scales with how long the license was lapsed.

Catch-Up CPE Required
  • 16 CPE hours on top of normal CPE OAR 801-010-0130: normal CPE hours for the lookback window PLUS a flat 16-hour penalty on top -- 96 total hours if lapsed less than 2 years, 176 total if lapsed 2-6 years, all within the 12 months before applying. Lapsed more than 6 years, reinstatement by CPE is unavailable.
  • 4 ethics hours, within your normal CPE requirement -- not the added catch-up hours above.
Source of record OAR 801-010-0130 (Reinstatement Requirements); OAR 801-010-0110 (Renewal); OAR 801-010-0010 (Fees) read the rule →

Sourcing note: The $225/$255 fee figures and the 16-hour CPE penalty are confirmed in the codified rule (OAR 801-010), but the Board's own License-Renewal-Information page describes the lapse/reinstatement mechanics without restating these dollar/hour figures itself. Recommend a manual check of the board's page if you want the numbers confirmed firsthand. (Last checked 2026-08-14.)

What triggers lapsed status: A license not renewed by the June 30 biennial deadline enters late-renewal status; if still not renewed within 60 days after the close of that permit period, it automatically lapses and can only be reinstated via a separate application, not renewed directly.
