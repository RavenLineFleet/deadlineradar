South Dakota CPA CPE Requirements: How Many Hours, By When

How much continuing professional education a South Dakota CPA actually needs — sourced the same way every fact on this site is: a board page, plus the codified rule itself where we could confirm it against primary law, clearly labelled where we could only confirm it against the board's own page, never a guess.

CPE Hour Requirement
120 hours every 3 years
  • 20-hour minimum in each 1-year period (you can't front-load the whole requirement into a single year).
Source of record SDCL 36-20B-27 read the rule →

Sourcing note: The 120-hour/3-year total is fully 2-sourced: SDCL 36-20B-27's primary text (fetched directly) states '120 hours of continuing education in each three-year renewal period,' matching the board's FAQ. The 20-hour annual minimum, though, is confirmed only on the board's own FAQ page, not independently in the statute text or in the administrative rule chapter (whose only annual-minimum language governs the initial-certificate phase-in population specifically, not general renewal) -- single-sourced, disclosed rather than silently treated as codified. (Last checked 2026-08-20.)

South Dakota CPAs on active status must complete 120 hours of CPE in each rolling three-year renewal period, with the board's own FAQ stating a minimum of 20 hours must be reported each year. There is no separate ongoing ethics-CPE-hour mandate -- South Dakota only requires the one-time AICPA ethics exam at initial licensure, not recurring ethics CPE. The correct general-renewal authority is SDCL 36-20B-27, not the administrative rule governing the first (initial-certificate phase-in) renewal only.
