Frequently asked questions

How does Tennessee's CPA license renewal cycle work?

Biennial (2-year) renewal covering both individual CPA/PA certificates and firm permits together on one shared cycle -- not two separate cycles. The Tennessee Board's guidance describes a biennial cycle with a shared expiration date and cohort (for example: a 12/31/2025 expiration, with the renewal window opening 11/2/2025 and a final late-renewal cutoff of 6/30/2026). Worth knowing: the currently effective codified rule still disagrees with this. Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0020-01-.11(2) reads 'All CPA and PA firm permits shall expire annually on December 31,' and the fee rule still prices firm permits per year -- while the Board's own current guidance runs firms on the shared biennial cycle described above. We follow the Board's live guidance, but confirm your firm's own expiration date with the Board rather than relying on either source alone.

How much does it cost to renew a CPA license in Tennessee?

$110. Flat $110 renewal fee (active status); $100 late fee if delinquent.

Does Tennessee require CPE hours to renew a CPA license?

Yes -- Tennessee requires 80 hours of CPE every 2 years. That includes 2 ethics hours. See the full Tennessee CPE requirements page for the exact citation.

What does it cost to reinstate a lapsed Tennessee CPA license?

$250. Board's reinstatement page states a $250 application fee for reinstatement of an expired individual or firm license.
